🚨 Scam Alerts

The most common Roblox trading scams. Learn them so they never get you.

🔐 "Give me your password"No one needs your password to trade. Anyone who asks is stealing your account.
🤝 "Trust trade" / "You go first"They promise to give it back or send theirs after. They won't. Only use the in-game trade window.
🔄 Last-second swapThey switch the item right before you accept. Always re-check both sides before you click accept.
🔗 Free item linksLinks to "free items" or "trade sites" steal accounts. Never click them.
💸 Real money offersSelling items for money or gift cards breaks Roblox rules and gets accounts banned.
🎭 Fake Lemon ManWe will never DM you asking for items, passwords, or money to "claim" a prize.

✅ Smart Trading Tips

Trade like a pro.

💎 Check values firstLook up both sides on the Value List before you agree.
